OUR JOURNEY & HISTORY

Located at Gresik, Indonesia, PT. Surabaya Mekabox began its first corrugated carton box factory in 1973. Since then, it has been continuously growing and moving forward with its first paper mill erection in 1985.

With current annual production capacity of 240,000 tonnes medium & test liner paper and 150,000 tonnes corrugated carton box, PT. Surabaya Mekabox has established long term partnerships with customers and suppliers from local and overseas.

ENVIRONMENT & SUSTAINABILITY

PT. Surabaya Mekabox supports environment sustainability by using waste paper for its raw material. With its years of experience, business ethic, and continuous technology development, it strives to become the preferred customer and supplier in the global packaging industry.
